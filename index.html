About

Born in Italy, named for Europe.

I was born in Italy to Chinese parents. My Chinese name is 飞欧 (Fēi Ōu): to fly and Europe, chosen by my grandfather when my parents moved here.

I'm a computer engineer from Genova. Since 2022 I've been an R&D software engineer at Ericsson, building cloud-native microservices and prototyping AI applications.

What I enjoy most is combining my passions with technology. My master's thesis applied AI to movement analysis for fitness, and the project I'm building now connects calisthenics parks around the world.

Now building

Prototype

CaliParksNextGen

Connecting public calisthenics parks to each other, so people can train together, meet, and build communities and challenges across the world.

Calisthenics is part of my life, and I've seen how a pull-up bar in a public park turns strangers into friends. CaliParksNextGen puts technology, AI and connectivity behind that feeling.

  • Train together

    Work out with people at other parks, wherever they are.

  • Meet

    Find the people who train at your park and the parks near you.

  • Community & challenges

    Grow communities around parks and challenge each other across cities.

Want to try it, or build it with me? Let's talk.
